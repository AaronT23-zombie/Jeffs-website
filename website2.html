    MISSION STATEMENT

    We accelerate the growth of individuals and empower teams to reach their full measure so they are prepared to face the Gettysburg tests that will happen in each of our lives. Be where you need to be when you need to be there because when the time of action has arrived, the time of preparation is over. No regrets, just results.

    HOW WE EXECUTE

    We do this through historical lesson take aways (on and off the battlefield), group discussions, learning how to measure teams and leaders, understanding the primary roles of leaders and subordinates, gaining communication and persuasion skills, establishing priorities, formulating a decision making process, perspective development, individual presentations, breakout sessions, tailored reading assignments, personal stories, team exercises and competitions, workshop activities, and by delivering specific takeaways that can be implemented immediately in the proper order. This allows teams and individuals to create their ‘Right Now Plan’ to effect the positive changes needed to accomplish the mission.
