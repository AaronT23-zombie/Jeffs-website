MISSION STATEMENT

We accelerate the growth of individuals and empower teams to reach their full measure so they are prepared to face the Gettysburg tests that will happen in each of our lives. Be where you need to be when you need to be there because when the time of action has arrived, the time of preparation is over. No regrets, just results.

HOW WE EXECUTE

We do this through historical lesson take aways (on and off the battlefield), group discussions, learning how to measure teams and leaders, understanding the primary roles of leaders and subordinates, gaining communication and persuasion skills, establishing priorities, formulating a decision making process, perspective development, individual presentations, breakout sessions, tailored reading assignments, personal stories, team exercises and competitions, workshop activities, and by delivering specific takeaways that can be implemented immediately in the proper order. This allows teams and individuals to create their ‘Right Now Plan’ to effect the positive changes needed to accomplish the mission.

LEADERSHIP

Jeffrey Utsch

Senior Faculty Lead Instructor at the Leadership and Freedom Center in Gettysburg, Pennsylvania, where he teaches leadership lessons learned from the battle and forging trusted teams and leaders. Jeff also teaches professional development and leading with a purpose courses to law enforcement, military and other agencies around the country. Jeff believes “Freedom is Not a Spectator Sport” and the more we understand our shared history the better we will govern ourselves. He has 32 years experience as President and CEO of a small company and 30 years’ experience as a Tactical Swim Instructor to Naval Special Warfare and other U.S. military personnel. Jeff was an NCAA-All American Swimmer and Captain of the University of Arizona Swim Team where he also earned a degree in Aerospace Engineering. He currently serves on the Board of the SEAL Veterans Foundation. JeffUtsch.com

Christopher Brownell

Exceptional organizational leader with over 32 years of experience in Special Operations, the last 15 years as a Command Master Chief in the SEAL Teams. He served as the NSW Group 2 CMC with direct leadership oversight on all aspects of personnel management, training, and logistics for all the east coast SEAL Teams and supporting commands, over 2800 personnel. Last served as the J3 Senior Enlisted Advisor to the General with direct oversight of all SOF Operations Globally. An effective and practiced communicator able to speak and write persuasively across demographic, geographic and cross-functional divides while delivering critical insightful analysis. Easily develops relationships to achieve consensus with foreign and domestic military, government officials and civilian leaders. Vice Chairman of the SEAL Legacy Foundation, a 501C(3) non-profit dedicated to supporting the families of the wounded and fallen United States Navy SEALs, which has given over $35 Million to the NSW Community over the past 13 years. Brownell holds a Master’s Degree in Organizational Leadership, a BS in Organizational Leadership, and has an Active (DoD) TS/SCI Security Clearance.
